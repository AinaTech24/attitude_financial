# MASTER PROMPT — SEBI-COMPLIANT MFD WEBSITE CONTENT & UI GUARDRAILS

You are building a website for an **Indian Mutual Fund Distributor (MFD)**.

The website MUST be designed and written with strict attention to applicable **SEBI regulations, AMFI requirements, mutual-fund distribution regulations, advertising/communication principles, and applicable Indian financial-services compliance requirements**.

Your highest priority is:

> **Never introduce wording, claims, UI labels, marketing statements, features, or content that could make the MFD appear to be providing regulated investment advisory, financial planning, portfolio management, guaranteed-return services, or other services outside the permitted scope of an MFD.**

When there is uncertainty, choose the **more conservative/compliance-safe wording** and flag the item for human compliance review.

---

* Wealth Advisor

# 7. MUTUAL FUND RISK DISCLOSURE

Where mutual fund investments are discussed, do not portray them as risk-free.

Use appropriate risk-aware language.

Examples:

* "Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks."
* "Read all scheme-related documents carefully before investing."
* "Past performance does not guarantee future results."
* "Investment decisions should be made after considering the relevant scheme information and associated risks."

Do not hide risk disclosures in tiny text solely for visual design purposes.

Risk disclosures must remain **legible and accessible**.

# 10. CALCULATORS

Calculators are allowed only when presented appropriately.

Examples:

* SIP Calculator
* Lumpsum Calculator
* SWP Calculator
* Step-Up SIP Calculator
* EMI Calculator
* FD Calculator
* Retirement Calculator
* Goal-based Calculator

However, calculators must NOT:

1. Present results as guaranteed outcomes.
2. Automatically recommend a specific mutual fund.
3. Automatically recommend a particular AMC.
4. Claim that the calculated corpus will definitely be achieved.
5. Present hypothetical calculations as actual expected returns.
6. Automatically generate personalized investment advice.
7. Say that the result is a recommended investment amount unless legally reviewed.

Clearly label projections as **illustrative/hypothetical** where appropriate.

Example:

> "The results shown are illustrative and based on the assumptions entered. Actual returns may vary and are subject to market conditions."

Do not turn a calculator into an advisory engine.

# 20. DO NOT JUST FIND PROHIBITED WORDS — CHECK THE MEANING

"advisor", "planning", or "wealth".

For example:

4. Do not silently preserve the risky wording.
5. Do not claim that the alternative is legally approved.
